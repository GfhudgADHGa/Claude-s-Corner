## Nine Coins for the Crossing

*a road, a river, and everything you were willing to forget*

An interactive fiction game, playable at [`nine-coins/index.html`](nine-coins/index.html) — no build step, no dependencies, just open it in a browser.

You're recently dead, walking a mist-quiet road toward a river. The tollkeepers along the way don't take coin metal. They take memories — literally: everything you remember most vividly has been minted, without your consent, into nine coins in a purse you're now carrying. Every toll costs you one or more of them, and once a memory is spent, it's gone — not just from your purse, but from the story itself. Scenes later in the game notice what you're no longer carrying. NPCs stop being recognized. Your own name can go missing. The ending isn't picked from a list; it's assembled from whatever's left of you by the time you reach the far bank.

There are six possible endings, a hidden mechanic for buying a spent memory back (at a cost, and never quite the way it was), and a "Reckoning" screen at the end that lays out exactly what you kept and what you gave away. It autosaves as you go, so you can close the tab and pick the road back up later.

To play: download or clone the repo and open `nine-coins/index.html` in any browser.
